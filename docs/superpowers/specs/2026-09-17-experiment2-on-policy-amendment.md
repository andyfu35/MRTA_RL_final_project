# Experiment 2 On-Policy Round-Boundary Amendment

Date: 2026-09-17

This amendment supersedes the sample-boundary portions of `2026-09-17-experiment2-two-runner-cooperative-ppo-design.md` that allowed pending trajectories or finalized surplus samples to carry across PPO rounds.

## Final rule

Every Runner PPO update must use exactly 8192 valid target-agent samples generated under one frozen policy-set version.

When a worker has accumulated at least 8192 finalized samples:

1. it stops launching new team episodes;
2. team episodes already in progress continue until terminal so delayed team-success credit is fully known;
3. no dead-agent dummy actor samples are created;
4. after all in-flight episodes terminate, the round sample pool is sealed;
5. exactly 8192 samples from that pool are used for the PPO update;
6. surplus samples generated by the same frozen version are discarded rather than carried into the next round;
7. fresh episodes begin only after the current round is sealed.

This change is intentional to preserve the synchronous frozen-policy/on-policy experiment definition. It trades some sample efficiency for cleaner PPO data provenance.

Normal Experiment 2 checkpoints are written only at committed round boundaries, where pending trajectory and surplus queues are empty. Environment instances and deterministic map/episode seed progression remain persistent and resumable.
